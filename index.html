DWG 01 / Overview

Ten years of turning “what the business needs” into “what gets shipped.”

I've spent the last decade-plus as the translator in the room — the person who sits between compliance officers, advisors, clinicians, and the engineers building for them, and comes out the other side with requirements nobody has to guess about. My background spans commercial & community banking, wealth management, healthcare operations, and retail, which means I've had to learn each domain's language before I could write a single user story in it.

Along the way I've led discovery on a $45B treasury platform migration, rebuilt an onboarding workflow that was quietly costing advisors 15 hours a week, and traced a $1.2M revenue leak back to a single data-mapping bug. The common thread: get close enough to the process to see what's actually broken, then document it clearly enough that a developer never has to ask "wait, what did they mean by this?"
